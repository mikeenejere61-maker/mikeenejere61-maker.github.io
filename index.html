Project 04 · Data Science

Fraud Detection Model

Pythonscikit-learnLogistic RegressionRandom Forest

Using the Project 01 transactions, I trained two machine-learning models to score each transaction for fraud risk. Fraud teams can only review a small share of transactions, so I tested the models on a realistic budget: review the riskiest 5% and see how much fraud that catches. Models were trained on 75% of the data and tested on the 15,000 transactions they had never seen.
